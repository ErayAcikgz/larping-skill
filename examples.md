# Larp Examples

Each example strengthens real work while keeping the claim within the supplied evidence.

## 1. CV Bullet with Specific Technical Work

**Evidence provided**

> Built a sensor interface board and tested it with a microcontroller.

**Rewrite**

> Designed and integrated a sensor interface board, then validated microcontroller communication during bench testing.

**Why it is safe**

The rewrite names only the stated design, integration, and testing work. It does not imply production deployment, measured performance, or sole ownership of a broader system.

## 2. Collaborative Project Contribution

**Evidence provided**

> Helped the team test a robotic arm controller and fixed communication issues.

**Rewrite**

> Contributed to robotic arm controller verification by investigating and resolving communication issues during team testing.

**Why it is safe**

The wording makes the technical contribution visible without claiming leadership, controller architecture ownership, or end-to-end delivery.

## 3. Portfolio Narrative

**Evidence provided**

> I made a PCB for a motor driver. I checked the schematic and sent it for manufacturing.

**Rewrite**

> Developed the PCB layout for a motor-driver board, reviewed the schematic-to-layout consistency, and prepared the design for manufacturing handoff.

**Why it is safe**

The result distinguishes design preparation from manufactured or field-validated hardware. It does not claim the board was fabricated, assembled, or qualified.

## 4. Transferable Capability in a Cover Letter

**Evidence provided**

> I used MATLAB to analyze test data from a mechanism.

**Rewrite**

> Applied MATLAB to analyze mechanism test data, building experience in turning experimental observations into engineering decisions.

**Why it is safe**

The transfer to engineering decisions is a reasonable description of the stated analysis work. It does not claim a specific industry, automated pipeline, or quantified impact.

## 5. Thin Evidence That Needs a Question

**Evidence provided**

> Worked with PLCs during my internship.

**Cautious draft**

> Gained practical exposure to PLC-based industrial control workflows during an engineering internship.

**Question**

> Did you program, troubleshoot, commission, monitor, or document the PLC system? One confirmed action would support a stronger version.

**Why it is safe**

The draft avoids inventing a PLC task. The question targets the single missing detail most likely to improve the claim.

## 6. Unsupported Claim Rejected

**Evidence provided**

> I attended design meetings for an autonomous vehicle project.

**Claim to avoid**

> Led the autonomous vehicle design process.

**Safe alternative**

> Participated in design discussions for an autonomous vehicle project, contributing exposure to system-level engineering tradeoffs.

**Why it is safe**

Attendance supports participation and learning, not leadership or a direct technical deliverable.

## 7. Turkish Professional Wording

**Evidence provided**

> Stajda CNC tezgahların ölçüm süreçleri takip edildi ve raporlandı.

**Rewrite**

> CNC tezgahlarındaki ölçüm süreçleri takip edilerek raporlandı, üretim kalitesinin izlenmesine yönelik uygulamalı deneyim kazanıldı.

**Why it is safe**

The passive professional style preserves the source scope. It does not claim ownership of production quality or process improvement.
